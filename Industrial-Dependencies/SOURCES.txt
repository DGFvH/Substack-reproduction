SOURCES OF THE ANALYSIS (analysis.R)
Industrial Dependencies, October 2026

The 9 active input files in Data/ are listed below with publisher, landing page, original download address,
licence and access date. Original dataset IDs are retained where still used in scripts and in the "dataset" column of
output/numbers.csv; removed sources leave gaps in the numbering.

Files marked [HAND-ENTERED] are manually transcribed snapshots, not automatically refreshed. Downloaded inputs are
either unchanged or locally filtered as specified below; the extracts are not pristine downloads. Filtering retained original
value strings, flags and period metadata; no price/time updates or aggregation were applied. Row counts exclude headers.
The two OECD manufacturing snapshots are combined with an added source_dataset column (D27/D31) to preserve provenance
and their different access dates. Original snapshots are retained under backup/pdf-audit-2026-10-23/ (ignored by Git).

Figure 1 uses related but not identical national production-volume index series. The annual percentage change is
calculated within each country's own series; it is not a comparison of index point levels. Different base years cancel
in that within-series ratio, but differences in frequency and seasonal/calendar adjustment remain, so small cross-country
differences should not be read as perfectly harmonized estimates.
  D25 Eurostat: annual manufacturing (NACE C) production-volume observations, unit I21 (2021=100), s_adj=NSA.
  D27/D31 OECD: manufacturing production-volume index, unit IX (2015=100), adjustment N; monthly except quarterly
  for Australia and New Zealand. The analysis uses the arithmetic mean of each year's monthly/quarterly observations.
  D22 Taiwan MOEA: monthly industrial production index, 110年=100 (2021=100); the analysis uses annual means.
  Netherlands: Figure 1 uses D25 Eurostat. The Dutch national comparison and Figure 2 use D6 CBS, a monthly
  seasonally and calendar-adjusted series; annual values there are arithmetic means. Their 2019-2025 changes therefore
  differ slightly (7.40% from Eurostat versus 7.63% from CBS for all manufacturing).
  South Korea: Figure 1 uses the OECD manufacturing production-volume index; Figure 2 separately shows a hand-entered
  production-capacity index. These are different measures.
